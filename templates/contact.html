Contact SwapDoc

The best way to reach us is by email: joe.bishop@swapdoc.co.uk. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Reporting a problem with a conversion

If a file didn't convert, or the result looks wrong, it helps to tell us:

  • which way you were converting (Word to PDF, or PDF to Word);
  • roughly how big the file was, and how many pages;
  • the message SwapDoc showed, if any;
  • what looked wrong (fonts, tables, pictures, page breaks and so on).

Please don't email us your document unless it contains nothing private and you're happy for us to look at it. We don't keep uploaded files, so we can't look at one you converted earlier.

Many layout problems have a quick fix. See Why formatting changes when you convert, and how to fix it.
