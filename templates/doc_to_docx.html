What to expect

DOC to DOCX

This direction is the easy one: .docx can hold everything a .doc can, so text, tables, pictures, headers, footers and comments all come across. Occasionally line spacing or a page break moves slightly, because newer Word lays documents out with updated rules.

DOCX to DOC

.doc is a 2003-era format, so anything newer has to be simplified. Ordinary documents convert fine. Features to check afterwards:

  • text effects such as glow, shadows and gradient fills;
  • content controls (fill-in boxes and drop-down lists in forms);
  • modern charts, SmartArt and icons, which may become plain pictures;
  • fonts the old format handles differently, especially in headings.

If something looks wrong, our guide to fixing formatting after converting covers the usual causes.

DOC to DOCX questions

How do I convert DOC to DOCX for free?
Drop the .doc into the box above and click Convert. No account needed, and the file is deleted after download.
Can I convert DOCX back to DOC?
Yes, with the same box. Drop a .docx and you'll get a Word 97-2003 .doc.
Will I lose anything converting DOCX to DOC?
Ordinary content is kept. Features newer than Word 2003 may be simplified (see above).
Are macros kept?
Don't rely on it. Keep your original file if it contains macros you need.
Is it safe to upload my document?
Files are sent over an encrypted connection, converted, and deleted as soon as your download finishes. Nobody reads them. See our Privacy Policy.
